## Overview

This project studies heart rate variability (HRV) as a physiological marker related to autonomic nervous system activity. The goal is to analyze HRV features from sensing data and connect them with health and behavioral contexts.

## Current Progress

I am working on HRV feature extraction, signal quality assessment, and analysis methods that make HRV measurements more reliable in mobile and real-world sensing settings.

## Preliminary Results

Preliminary analysis indicates that HRV features can provide useful information about physiological regulation, but reliable estimation depends strongly on signal quality and careful preprocessing.
