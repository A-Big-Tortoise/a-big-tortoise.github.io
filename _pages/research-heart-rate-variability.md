## Overview

This project studies reliable heart rate variability (HRV) estimation from noisy ballistocardiography/bodyseismography (BCG/BSG) signals. The goal is to extract fine-grained HRV metrics that reflect autonomic nervous system activity, even when the underlying sensing data is affected by motion artifacts, weak cardiac components, and real-world noise.

## Current Progress

I am working on signal quality assessment, beat-to-beat interval extraction, artifact handling, and HRV feature analysis methods that make HRV measurements more reliable from noisy BCG/BSG signals.

## Preliminary Results

Preliminary analysis indicates that fine-grained HRV features can be recovered from BCG/BSG signals under selected conditions, but reliable estimation requires careful preprocessing, noise rejection, and validation against higher-quality physiological references.
