I developed a contactless seismic-sensing system that estimates heart rate, respiratory rate, and heart rate variability from sensors beneath more than ten types of office chairs. The work drew laboratory visits from senior vice presidents of MillerKnoll, one of the world's largest furniture manufacturers, which placed a pilot order for 15 units, with testing now underway.
