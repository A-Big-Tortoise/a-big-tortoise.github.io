## Overview

This project explores low-burden methods for monitoring infant vital signs. The goal is to estimate physiological indicators such as respiration and heart-related activity while reducing the need for intrusive wearable sensors.

## Current Progress

I am developing sensing and signal processing methods for extracting vital-sign-related patterns from noisy real-world data. Current work focuses on improving signal quality, handling motion artifacts, and evaluating the feasibility of continuous monitoring.

## Preliminary Results

Initial experiments suggest that non-contact or minimally intrusive sensing can provide useful vital-sign-related signals under controlled conditions. Ongoing work is focused on improving reliability in more realistic scenarios.
