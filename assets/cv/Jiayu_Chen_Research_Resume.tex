\sectiontitle{Research Experience}
\entryhead{\textbf{University of Georgia} \textbar{} Ph.D. \textbar{} Athens, GA \textbar{} Aug 2024 - Present}
\begin{tightitems}
  \item \textbf{Respiratory \& Sleep Monitoring:} Developed the core contactless respiratory-rate (RR) algorithm for FDA-cleared BedDot (G1). Developed SeismoApnea, a contact-free tri-axial geophone system for overnight sleep-apnea monitoring that estimates total sleep time (TST), Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI), and four-class apnea severity. Across 80 overnight recordings from 80 subjects, it achieved an AHI ICC of 0.870 and a severity macro F1 of 73.11\%. FDA clearance is being pursued specifically for AHI estimation.
  \item \textbf{Cardiovascular Monitoring:} Developed and deployed \href{https://doi.org/10.1145/3770701}{SelfDenoiser}, a self-supervised temporal-spectral framework trained on 11,392 hours of ICU BCG recordings, as the core denoising module for contactless cardiac monitoring. The module improves downstream heart-rate and inter-beat-interval estimation and is part of a filed FDA submission.
  \item \textbf{Office Health Monitoring:} Developed a contactless vital-sign monitoring system for real-world office environments, achieving heart-rate and respiratory-rate MAEs of approximately 3.9 bpm and 2.4 breaths/min, with 88\% and 21\% temporal coverage, respectively. Following an on-site visit led by a MillerKnoll Senior Vice President, the company procured 30 units for pilot testing.
  \item \textbf{Infant Monitoring:} Data collection is underway at Children's Healthcare of Atlanta (CHOA) using a contactless tri-axis geophone system in bassinet, incubator, and crib settings. Preliminary signal-quality analysis demonstrates the feasibility of monitoring occupancy, heart rate, respiratory rate, sleep/wake states, and crying.
\end{tightitems}
